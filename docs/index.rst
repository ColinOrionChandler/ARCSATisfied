ARCSATisfied
============

Quick CCD reduction and catalog enrichment for APO ARCSAT FITS data.

ARCSATisfied catalogs a night, combines available bias and dark calibrations,
constructs per-filter flats, and reduces science frames. It can enrich the
catalog with JPL Horizons ephemerides and solve WCS with ASTAP.
